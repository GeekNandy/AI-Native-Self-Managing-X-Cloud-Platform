A WORKING VERTICAL SLICE

AI proposes. The control plane decides.

This proof of concept translates structured requirements into deterministic plans, generated workload files and a capability-checked local runtime.

  • Offline mode: exact replay of curated examples, with no model calls.
  • Gemini mode: structured extraction with timeouts and bounded concurrency. It cannot approve or execute plans.
  • Runtime: durable simulator by default, or isolated Docker development workloads. Unsupported provider requirements are denied.
  • Feedback: reviewed corrections become regression cases. No autonomous model training or policy changes.

Single-node PoC with durable state, scoped access, repository repair and MCP. Cloud IAM, multi-tenant identity and distributed coordination remain future work.
